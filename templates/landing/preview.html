Frequently asked questions

The short answers. The long ones live in the docs, linked from each.

Can I change plans later?
Yes — up or down, whenever. Changes apply from the next billing cycle, and nothing about your data or settings moves.
What counts as a seat?
Anyone with their own login. Shared logins are not a loophole we police; they are a security problem we will help you fix.
Do you offer refunds?
Within 30 days, in full, no questions and no exit interview. After that, write to us — a human reads it.
Where is my data stored?
In the region you pick at signup, and it stays there. Exports are complete and free, including the day you leave.
